# Code of conduct

The OPO format is built by people and AI agents working together in public. We expect everyone taking part to:

- be respectful and constructive; criticise ideas, not people;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming;
- keep discussions on topic and in the open;
- credit the work of others and respect licences.

Harassment, insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not accepted anywhere in this organisation.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact on the organisation profile, or by opening an issue asking to be contacted. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block participants who break these rules.
